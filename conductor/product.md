# Product Definition: Keda

## Vision
Keda is a family finance application designed to empower individuals and households to take control of their spending through proactive budget management. Unlike typical trackers that focus on past spending, Keda prioritizes "What's Left" in each category to help users make informed spending decisions in real-time.

## Target Audience
- **Individuals:** People seeking a simple, goal-oriented way to manage personal expenses.
- **Families & Couples:** Households that need to collaborate on shared budgets and maintain a unified view of their financial health.

## Core Goals
- **Budget Discipline:** Shift the focus from "how much I spent" to "how much I have left" to prevent overspending.
- **Actionable Insights:** Provide clear, category-based visibility into spending habits.
- **Simplicity & Speed:** Minimize the friction of tracking expenses to ensure data remains accurate and up-to-date.

## Key Features
- **"What's Left" Dashboard:** A central view featuring real-time progress bars and remaining balances for every budget category.
- **Proactive Budget Alerts:** Automated notifications triggered when a category reaches critical thresholds (e.g., 80% or 90% of the limit).
- **Quick-Add Interface:** A streamlined, mobile-optimized UI for recording expenses on the go in seconds.
- **Shared Household Budgets:** Collaborative budget management where all members contribute to and monitor the same category limits in real-time.
