# Product Guidelines: Keda

## Brand Personality & Tone
- **Friendly & Encouraging:** Financial tracking can be stressful. Keda should feel like a supportive coach. Use positive reinforcement and approachable language (e.g., "You've got this!" or "Great job staying on track!").
- **Clarity Over Jargon:** Avoid complex financial terminology. Use simple, everyday language that anyone can understand.
- **Empowering:** Focus on what the user *can* do with their remaining budget, rather than just what they've already spent.

## Visual Identity
- **Clean & Modern:** Prioritize white space and a minimalist layout to reduce cognitive load. The UI should feel light and airy, not cramped or overwhelming.
- **Scannable Hierarchy:** Use clear typography and consistent spacing to ensure the most important information—"What's Left"—is always the first thing a user sees.
- **Intentional Color:** Use color purposefully to indicate status (e.g., green for healthy balances, amber for approaching limits) rather than for pure decoration.

## Messaging Pillars
- **Empowerment:** "Your money, your decisions." Remind users that the tool is there to give them data so they can make choices that align with their goals.
- **Simplicity:** "Manage your money in seconds, not hours." Highlight how Keda fits into a busy lifestyle through its quick-add interface and at-a-glance dashboard.
